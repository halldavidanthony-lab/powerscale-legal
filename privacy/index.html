Privacy Policy

Effective: June 26, 2026  ·  Last updated: June 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information PowerScale ("we", "us", "our") collects when you use the PowerScale mobile app ("App"), how we use it, and your rights. We take your health data seriously and have built PowerScale around a local-first data model — your fitness data lives on your device by default.

1. Who We Are

PowerScale is operated by David Hall. For privacy questions, contact us at davidahall15@yahoo.com.

2. Data We Collect

Account information

  • Email address (used to identify your account)
  • Display name (chosen by you)
  • Subscription tier (free or PEAK)

Fitness & progression data

  • Workout plans, completed sessions, and training history
  • In-app progression metrics: rank, experience points (EXP), world assignment
  • Goals, questlines, and cosmetic preferences

This data is stored on your device. If you enable server sync, it is also uploaded to our secure backend so you can restore it on a new device.

Photos & videos (optional, with explicit consent)

  • A selfie or photo you choose to add for your in-app avatar.
  • Workout-form videos you choose to submit for PEAK Form-Check coaching review.
  • PowerScale v1 has no public photo or video sharing — this content is used only to provide the feature you submitted it for and is never posted to other users.
  • You can revoke photo consent at any time in Settings → Privacy. Revoking consent removes photos from local storage immediately.

Apple HealthKit data

  • If you grant HealthKit permission, we may read workout activity, heart rate, and body measurements to auto-populate your training log.
  • We do not sell, share, or use HealthKit data for advertising.
  • HealthKit data is never uploaded to our servers; it stays on your device.
  • You can revoke HealthKit access at any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Usage & preferences

  • In-app preferences: unit system (metric/imperial), language
  • Standard crash logs and anonymous diagnostics (via Apple's built-in crash reporting). These do not include your health data.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and improve the App's features
  • To sync your data across devices when you opt in to server sync
  • To process and manage your subscription through the Apple App Store
  • To respond to your support requests
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not use your data to serve targeted ads, sell it to third parties, or use it for any purpose unrelated to operating PowerScale.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only in these limited cases:

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit — handles subscription billing. Apple's privacy policy governs that data.
  • Hosting & backend infrastructure — if you enable sync, data is stored on our backend servers under our control. We use industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, court order, or to protect rights and safety.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Your local data stays on your device until you delete it. If you enabled server sync, your server-side data is retained until you delete your account.

To delete everything: go to Settings → Privacy → "Delete account + all data". This wipes all local data and queues a deletion request for any server-side data. Server deletion is completed within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

PowerScale is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact davidahall15@yahoo.com and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

All users can:

  • Access and export their data (contact us)
  • Delete all data via the in-app delete function
  • Opt out of photo processing and server sync at any time

EU / UK residents (GDPR / UK GDPR) additionally have the right to: rectification, restriction of processing, portability, and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

California residents (CCPA / CPRA) have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion, and opt out of sale (we do not sell personal data).

Washington State residents (My Health My Data Act — MHMDA): we obtain your affirmative consent before collecting or sharing consumer health data. You may withdraw consent and request deletion as described above.

8. Security

We use TLS encryption for all data in transit. Local data is protected by your device's built-in encryption. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we follow industry best practices.

9. Third-Party Links

The App may contain links to third-party sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will notify you of material changes via the App or by email. Continued use after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.
